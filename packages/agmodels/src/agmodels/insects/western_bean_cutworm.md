A quarter of the moth flight is expected by about 1,320 degree days (°F) after May 1, half by about 1,420 and three quarters by about 1,535 (Ahmad 1979, as reported by Hanson et al. 2015). Moths keep flying and laying eggs after 1,535, though the model's risk ends there.
