In Wisconsin, overwintering adult Spotted Wing Drosophila begin egg-laying in mid to late June (around 1276 FDD), and will complete a generation every 8-12 days. Populations peak around 3100 FDD, and will continue to be a threat to soft-skinned fruit through the end of the season.
