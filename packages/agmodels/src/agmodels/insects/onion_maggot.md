First generation adult flight peaks around 680 FDD and second generation adult flight peaks around 1950. Sprays against the adult flies seldom prevent damage; protection comes from seed or in-furrow treatments at planting, and the [onion maggot topic page](/topics/onion-maggot) covers management.
