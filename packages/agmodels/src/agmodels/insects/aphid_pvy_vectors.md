The risk of PVY transmission by aphid vectors begins around 1838 FDD, peaks around 2515 FDD, and remains elevated through vine kill. PVY transmission risk is calculated from a combination of several aphid species, the relative abundance of each species in Wisconsin, and the estimated PVY transmission efficiency of each species. Aphid vectors include Soybean aphid, Bird cherry-oat aphid, Green peach aphid, Pea aphid, Corn leaf aphid, and Potato aphid.
