## Model details

The early blight model is based on the accumulation of potato physiological days (P-days) as described in Pscheidt and Stevenson 1988. Risk is based on cumulative P-days since crop emergence, with the highest risk (and when initial fungicide applications should be targeted) from 300 cumulative P-days (High from 300, Very high from 350).
