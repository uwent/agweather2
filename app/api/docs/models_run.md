# Run a model

A model's daily results at one location, or at several, from its biofix or your planting date (January 1 for a model with neither) through the forecast. Each row is one day: `date`, `kind`, the weather the model uses, its results, and where the model defines them, `risk_level` and `risk_label`.

The model's options, such as a crop's maturity group or an irrigation setting, are added as parameters by name (`/models/{model_id}` lists them, as `maturity_group=2.5`). Giving `all` for one option runs every choice, up to 20 at a time, with an `option_set` column identifying each.

With a `planting_date`, a disease model also runs the crop's growth stages and adds a `susceptibility` column for the period when the crop can be infected. The wheat scab model takes a spring wheat planting date this way, and also a `flowering_date` (YYYY-MM-DD), the day your wheat began flowering, which sets that period itself for winter or spring wheat: from that day through 7 days after. With `projection`, the results continue to the end of the season using the weather of each earlier year in the archive, and `meta.summary.projection` gives the range of dates on which each stage or risk level may arrive. Without it, `meta.summary.milestones` gives the day each stage and risk level was first reached through the forecast.

To find days in the results, `first=high` gives the first day the risk was High or higher, `last=high` the last such day, and `since=high` the number of days since then. A crop model takes a stage's code, as `first=R1` for the first day at R1 or later. Any result takes bounds after its name, as `first=dd_total:above_450`, in the units it is shown in, and the risk level takes them alone: `first=above_2` is the first day at the third level or higher. An insect's life stage is a number for now (`first=stage:above_3`). The days looked through are the rows returned; `since` counts to `end`, or to today when `end` is left out. The answers are in `meta.summary.events`.

For several locations, give `points` in place of `lat` and `lng`. The results come back as one table, with a `point` column saying which location each row is for, and the same options apply at every location. A typical planting date (`early`, `mid` or `late`) and a biofix set by the crop's growth can fall on different days at each location; `meta.summary.points` lists them. A request with `projection` can include up to 10 locations; for more, make one request per location.

When the weather a model reads is missing for a day, we estimate it from the days on either side, for gaps of up to seven days, and mark the day in a column ending `_estimated` for that input (`air_temperature_max_estimated`). A longer gap is left missing: the model's running totals, such as degree days or disease severity values, add nothing for those days, so its results after them may be late. A column ending `_missing` marks those days for each input (`air_temperature_max_missing`), and a warning says how many there were, counting any before the first day returned that the results depend on. Days after the weather ends, such as the rest of today, are not counted.

A model's results are guidance based on weather data, not a prediction of what will happen in a particular field. Check the model's region and season in `/models/{model_id}`.

## In meta.summary

- `days_returned`, `days_forecast` and `days_missing`: The number of rows returned, how many are forecast days, and how many have no data.
- `estimated_by_input`: For each input the model needed an estimate for, the number of rows on which it was estimated.
- `missing_by_input`: For each input with days missing in gaps longer than seven days, the number of those days the results depend on, including any before the first day returned. Only present when there are any.
- `days_projected`: With `projection`, the number of rows past the forecast.
- `windows`: For an insect, each action window's periods as pairs of first and last days, by window name. With several locations, option sets or projected years, each entry names its `point`, `option_set` or `year`.
- `milestones`: Without `projection`, for each crop stage, risk level above the lowest and action window (`what` and `name`), the first day it was reached (`date`) through the forecast, or null if it wasn't, the kind of that day (`day_kind`: `observed`, `forecast` and so on), and `before_start`, true when it was reached on the first day returned and may have been reached earlier. With several option sets, a list for each; with several locations, this is in `points`.
- `events`: For each `first`, `last` and `since` asked for, what was found: the `event` as given, its `kind`, the result it looks at (`output`), the `date` found (or `days` since it, for `since`), null if none, its `day_kind` and `before_start` as in `milestones`, and `missing_days`, the days without a value. With `projection`, a `first` also has `projection`: in how many of the earlier years it is reached (`reached` of `of`) and the 10th, 50th and 90th percentile dates (`p10`, `p50`, `p90`). With several option sets, a list for each; with several locations, this is in `points`.
- `option_sets`: When an option is given as `all`, the option values of each `option_set`.
- `projection`: With `projection`, how it was made (`mode`), the first projected day (`start`), the earlier years used (`years`), and `milestones`: for each crop stage, risk level and action window (`what` and `name`), in how many of the years it was reached (`reached` of `of`) and the 10th, 50th and 90th percentile dates (`p10`, `p50`, `p90`). With several locations, this is in `points`.
- `points`: With several locations, what was worked out at each: its `biofix`, its `planting_date`, its `option_sets` where they differ between locations, its `projection`, `milestones` and `events`.
- `corrections`: The corrections we applied to the weather we read, each with its version, such as our calibration of the forecast's temperatures.
- `season`: For a model counted over a season, such as a winter: the season's `name` and `label`, its `first` and `last` days, the day its map is first shown (`appears`), the last day counted so far (`through`), and whether the count is `final`.
