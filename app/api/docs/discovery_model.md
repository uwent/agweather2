# One model

A model's full description: what it predicts, the daily weather it needs and the hourly variables those come from, its results and their units, its options (such as a biofix date or a crop's maturity group), how its risk levels are defined, and its version history.

This is everything needed to run the model on weather from your own station with `POST /models/{model_id}/run`, or to tell whether your station records the variables the model needs.
