# Risk today

Each model's risk at one location on one day (today unless you choose another), as a list with a row for each model: its name, group and version, the day's `kind`, `risk_level`, `risk_label`, and with a `planting_date` the crop's `susceptibility`.

Without `models`, the list includes every model that is in season and was developed for the location's region. When you name models, each one is listed, and `reason` explains why a model reports no risk on that day.
