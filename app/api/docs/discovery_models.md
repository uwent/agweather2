# All models

Every model the service runs, with a short description, its group (disease, insect or crop), the crops it applies to, the region and season it was developed for, its status and version, and the research it is based on.

A model's status is `stable`, or `beta` while it is new or under review and its results are more likely to change. Each model's version changes when its results change, and `/models/{model_id}` lists what changed.
