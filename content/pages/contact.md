We welcome questions, problem reports and suggestions about AgWeather. Email us at [agweather@cals.wisc.edu](mailto:agweather@cals.wisc.edu), and we will answer as soon as we can.

## What to include

A few details help us answer you quickly:

- **The page's address,** copied from your browser, or the location and date you were looking at.
- **What you expected** and what you saw instead. A screenshot helps.
- **For a model's results,** the model, the location and the planting date, biofix or other settings you used.
- **For the API,** the request you sent and the response you got back.
- **Your browser and device,** if a page didn't work as it should.

For questions about managing a crop, disease or pest on your farm, your county's extension office can also help, and it knows local conditions.

## Using the API

If you use AgWeather's data through the API, please [tell us how you use it](/api-use). It helps us plan changes and lets us tell you before one affects your work.

## What's new

The [changelog](/changelog) lists what we have added, fixed and changed, and announces planned changes and outages. You can follow it with a feed reader.
