# Open-Meteo's humidity outside Wisconsin

Hand-written, started 2026-11-19 (D100). The Wisconsin comparison of Open-Meteo with Wisconet, and the fit of D51's humidity map, are in `openmeteo.md` (generated by `scripts/compare_openmeteo_wisconet.py`); this report records how Open-Meteo's humidity compares with stations and with URMA elsewhere, and the checks behind any correction for points outside the archive. The plan for the work still to do is `docs/openmeteo-humidity-plan.md`.

Disease models count hours with RH at or above 90 % as a field station measures it. On gridded or modelled humidity those hours are counted at a lower threshold (D50: 88 % on the archive, matched to 80 Wisconet stations). Points outside the archive read Open-Meteo, and D51 mapped its RH onto the archive's with a table fitted at Wisconet's stations' cells.

## Five Oklahoma Mesonet stations, 2023–2025

`tmp/pecan-check/report.md` (scripts and full tables beside it). The Mesonet's 5-minute observations are copyrighted and redistribution is restricted, so the files stay in `tmp/` and no station series is kept in the repository.

- **Stations:** Okmulgee (OKMU), Burneyville (BURN), Durant (DURA), Eufaula (EUFA) and Shawnee (SHAW), in east- and south-central Oklahoma's pecan country; March 1 to August 31 of 2023, 2024 and 2025 (15 station-seasons, 2,760 daily files); top-of-hour observations at 1.5 m, hours a station lacks left out of every source.
- **Open-Meteo:** the historical-forecast endpoint at each station (best_match), v2's own fetch (`agdata.sources.openmeteo.fetch`, NBM then best_match; NBM supplied 2025, best_match 2023–2024), the same after D51's map, and the archive endpoint's ERA5 and best_match.
- **Measure:** hours with RH ≥ 90 % and temperature ≥ 70 °F (pecan scab's scab hour); the threshold at which a source's season count equals the station's, interpolated between whole percents.

| Source | Hours at 90 %, pooled ratio to the stations | Matching threshold, pooled | Station-seasons (interquartile range) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Historical forecast (best_match) | 0.68 | 87.6 % | 86.5–88.7 % |
| v2's fetch, raw | 0.67 | 87.5 % | 86.7–88.4 % |
| v2's fetch after D51's map | 1.12 | 90.7 % | 89.8–91.6 % |
| ERA5 | 0.54 | 86.6 % | 84.7–88.4 % |

- **By month** (historical forecast, matching threshold): April 90.3 %, May 89.8 %, June 88.2 %, July 86.9 %, August 86.0 %. Open-Meteo is about as humid as the stations in spring and drier in summer.
- **D51's map in Oklahoma** made April 3.7 and May 2.2 times too humid (hours at 90 %), so scab-hour totals reached 10, 20 and 30 hours a median 15 days early. On mapped RH, D50's 88 % counted 1.45 times the stations' RH ≥ 90 % hours.
- **The advisor's spray rule**, simulated (10 hours; 30 days after March 1, 10 after a spray): the stations call for 9.7 sprays a season; raw Open-Meteo at 88 % 10.0, the first spray a median 2 days early.
- **Daily agreement:** the forecast-based series follow the stations' days (daily correlation 0.84 at 90 %); the reanalyses don't (0.58–0.66).

So D100: D51's map applies only inside `openmeteo.RH_MAP_AREA` (40–49.5° N, 97.5–82° W), and elsewhere Open-Meteo's RH is read as it comes, counted at D50's 88 %. For comparison, raw Open-Meteo matched Wisconet's 90 % at about 85 % (`openmeteo.md`, NBM), so a single threshold for raw Open-Meteo is about 3 points off in one place or the other. The box is a stopgap; the plan replaces it.
