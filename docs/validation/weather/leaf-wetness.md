# Leaf wetness estimated from weather, against NEWA's orchard sensors

Generated 2026-11-18 by `scripts/compare_leaf_wetness.py` (`docs/plans/models.md` part B5), on the hours of 2024–2026. Apple scab and fire blight need the hours a leaf is wet, which the archive has no sensor for, so the models estimate wetness from humidity, dew point, wind and rain. This compares 60 candidate estimates with the leaf wetness sensors at the Wisconsin orchard stations of NEWA (the Network for Environment and Weather Applications, Cornell University; hourly station data from newa.cornell.edu), to choose the estimate the models use and to measure its errors.

The truth is each NEWA station's leaf wetness sensor (an hour wet at 30 minutes or more); only hours the station observed are scored. Each estimate runs on the station's own hours and on the archive's hours at the station's cell. Event scores are Cohen's kappa over station-days: apple scab infection events by NEWA's rules (both sides use the station's rain and temperature on station inputs, the archive's on grid inputs; the truth always uses the sensor with the station's rain and temperature) and fire blight wetting days (2 wet hours or more).

86 station-seasons: wi_bai, wi_bel, wi_bla, wi_blm, wi_bri, wi_brk, wi_bur, wi_chi, wi_chl, wi_col, wi_eau, wi_ecl, wi_fat, wi_gaf, wi_gal, wi_gay, wi_gen, wi_gms, wi_mal, wi_mau, wi_meq, wi_mer, wi_mho, wi_mth, wi_ply, wi_pow, wi_ric, wi_roc, wi_spv, wi_tre, wi_ver, wi_wab, wi_wau.
