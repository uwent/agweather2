Every endpoint in `docs/api.md`, built in the order below, so that each step serves something real as soon as it lands. Weather at points and degree days come early (C): they're what v1's callers (DATCP, Veg Path) use, and the 2027 season is their deadline. **The interface it serves (Ben, 2026-10-30).** The main view is the Crop Risk Tool's, on a map. A model selector. A map showing the model's output as a gridded layer, with the model's name and the date across the top and a date slider along the bottom that scrubs back through the archive and forward through the forecast from today. Clicking the map drops a pin and highlights the cell, and a time series chart for that point opens on the right: the model's input weather, its output, or several outputs at once (every corn maturity group when none is chosen). A point can be pinned. Pinned sites list in a left sidebar and persist in the browser, or in the account when signed in, and their charts stack as cards in a feed on the right. The same map and slider show weather (hourly and daily variables, running totals) and climate (normals, current against normal). A seasonal product is one map per season (Phase 4 H), chosen by season rather than scrubbed by day.

**What exists:** weather maps by date (`series.grid_day`, `grid_total`: one read per map day, archive then forecast); points with every model through the forecast (`model_at_point`, under a second); per-point series with kinds and sources (`point_days`, `point_hours`); the runner (`agmodels.runner.run`, `required_inputs`); the hourly → daily rollup at a point (`agdata.point.daily_at_point`); units (`agdata.units`); QC ranges (`agdata.qc`); the model registry (`agmodels.registry`).
